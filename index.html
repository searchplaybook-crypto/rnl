Company Background & Heritage

Welcome to R&L Books

A proud division of the R & L Group — a multidisciplinary South African enterprise holding key interests in Academic Publishing, Book Distribution, Legal Library Services, Digitization, Online Licenses, Computer Software, and Supply License Management.

Established in 2006 & Headquartered at UKZN Howard College

Operating continuously since 2006, R&L Books operates its primary campus bookshop flagship at UKZN Howard College (Mazisi Kunene Rd, Durban, KZN), serving law firms, advocate chambers, academic faculties, and university students.

We understand that in the legal and academic arenas, access to accurate, timely, and organized information is paramount. "Justice delayed is justice denied." We know the legal landscape, we know the market, and we understand the exacting demands of legal professionals, judiciary members, university faculties, and students.

With a vast pool of resources, information, and experienced law librarians, R&L Books delivers integrated solutions — bridging traditional loose-leaf physical publishing updates with modern digital software supply, open-access legal indices, and e-learning platforms.

Our Mission Statement

"To empower South African legal professionals, academic institutions, law firms, and students with reliable, meticulously updated legal libraries, prescribed learning materials, and seamless digital licensing solutions — driven by uncompromising accuracy, service integrity, and genuine black empowerment."

Specialized Field Services

Legal Library Management Framework

Comprehensive loose-leaf updating, law report annotations, statute maintenance, and complete library setups across South Africa's major legal publishing houses.

Loose-Leaf Updating & Filing

Our qualified legal librarians possess over 48 years of combined law library experience. Each librarian is trained to provide efficient filing of loose-leaf updates across all major legal publishing houses (Juta, LexisNexis, etc.).

  • Withdrawal of outdated & superseded issues
  • Insertion of missing service updates & page checks
  • Scheduled monthly librarian site visits
  • Streamlined indexing for immediate research

Law Report Annotations

Save invaluable research time with meticulous law report annotations. We annotate your South African Law Reports and provide relevant judicial decisions to date.

Annotation Timings:
• South African Law Reports: Bi-monthly
• Criminal Law Reports: Bi-monthly
• Industrial Law Journals: Bi-monthly
• Corrigenda: Monthly

New Library Setup & Backlog Clearance

Complete library organization for new advocate chambers or law firms. Clearing backlogs of accumulated service issues, sorting Government Gazettes numerically, and establishing firm-branded indexing.

  • Index cards & envelope systems in textbooks
  • Subject markings on spines and shelf areas
  • Books stamped with firm ownership name
  • Admin files & bookkeeper invoice audits
