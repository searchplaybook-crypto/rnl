Company Background & Heritage

Built around books. Evolving with information.

R&L Books is a division of the R&L Group, with interests spanning academic publishing, book distribution, legal library services, digitisation, online licences, computer software and related supply services.

Established in 2006

R&L Books has operated since 2006, developing practical supply and information services for academic, legal, institutional and corporate environments. The current operational base is at UKZN Howard College in Durban.

The business sits at the intersection of physical and digital information. Traditional legal collections still require disciplined updating, filing and maintenance, while institutions increasingly require digitised collections, e-learning content, software, licences and online access.

This allows R&L Books to support the information lifecycle rather than treating books, libraries and digital platforms as separate activities.

Our operating principles

Legal Library Updating

Keeping legal collections current, organised and usable.

Legal reference collections do not end with delivery. Updating, filing, indexing and maintenance keep the collection useful over time.

Physical library maintenance

R&L Books supports legal libraries with the operational work required to maintain loose-leaf and reference collections, including new library setup and the clearing of accumulated filing backlogs.

  • Loose-leaf insertions, filing and collection checks
  • Annotations, corrigenda and noter-up services
  • New legal library setup and organisation
  • Backlog filing and collection recovery
  • Law reports, journals, statutes and government gazettes
  • Ongoing library updating and maintenance support

Loose-Leaf Updating

Structured insertion and filing of updates into existing loose-leaf legal collections.

Annotations & Noter-Up

Maintenance work that helps keep reported cases and reference material properly updated.

Library Setup

Organisation and preparation of new legal reference collections for institutional use.

Backlog Filing
