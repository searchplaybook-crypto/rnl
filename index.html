◆ Company Background & Heritage

Built around books. Evolving with information.

R&L Books is a division of the R&L Group, with interests spanning academic publishing, book distribution, legal library services, digitisation, online licences, computer software and related supply services.

Established in 2006

R&L Books has operated since 2006, developing practical supply and information services for academic, legal, institutional and corporate environments. The current operational base is at UKZN Howard College in Durban.

The business sits at the intersection of physical and digital information. Traditional legal collections still require disciplined updating, filing and maintenance, while institutions increasingly require digitised collections, e-learning content, software, licences and online access.

This allows R&L Books to support the information lifecycle rather than treating books, libraries and digital platforms as separate activities.

Academic Books & Publishing Supply

  • ✓Institutional book supply for faculties, libraries and departments
  • ✓Publishing and distribution relationships with local and international publishers

  • ✓Supply to nursing colleges, schools, government and corporate institutions

Where this fits

Academic supply is more than filling an order. Institutions need reliable sourcing, accurate edition matching, and a supplier that understands the difference between a prescribed textbook and a general trade book. R&L Books operates at that intersection — connecting publishers, libraries and learners with the right information at the right time.

Legal Library Services

Legal reference collections require ongoing maintenance. R&L Books provides the operational services that keep loose-leaf sets, law reports, journals, statutes and gazettes current, filed and usable.

  • ✓Loose-leaf updating — insertion and filing of amendments and new material
  • ✓Annotations and noter-up work on reported cases and reference sources
  • ✓Legal library setup — organising and preparing new collections for institutional use
  • ✓Backlog filing — systematic recovery of accumulated inserts and outstanding updates
  • ✓Law reports, legal journals and government gazettes
  • ✓Ongoing updating and maintenance support, including daily updating where required

Why it matters

A legal library that is not updated is a liability, not an asset. Every day that inserts sit unfiled is a day the collection cannot be relied on. R&L Books treats legal library maintenance as a continuous discipline, not a one-off delivery. That is what makes a collection usable by practitioners, faculties and researchers.

Digitisation, E-Learning & Digital Supply

Institutional information is moving into digital environments. R&L Books supports that transition with digitisation, e-learning content, online licences, software supply and digital resource management — as an evolution of the physical collection, not a replacement for it.

  • ✓Digitisation of physical collections, documents and reference material
  • ✓E-learning content and platform support for academic and training environments
  • ✓Online licences and digital resource access for libraries and institutions
  • ✓Software supply and licence management for academic and professional use
  • ✓Digital supply of course materials, reference content and subscription resources
  • ✓Ongoing maintenance of both physical and digital reference environments

The direction

Physical books remain essential where institutions require authoritative, archived and authenticated reference collections. At the same time, learning, research and legal practice increasingly depend on digital access. R&L Books positions itself to support both — so that institutions do not have to choose between heritage and modernisation.
